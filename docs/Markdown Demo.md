## Bold and Italics

#### Italics: 
*These words are important* - use the asterisk and put a word or sentence between the two asterisks

#### Bold:
**This sentence is bold** - uses the 2 asterisks and then word or sentence followed by 2 more asterisks

#### Bold + Italic:

***This sentence is super important and bold*** - uses 3 asterisks word or sentence followed by 3 more asterisks
